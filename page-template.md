## The 60-Second Answer (Top of Page, Above the Fold)

> The Godrej 1% Plan breaks a home purchase into five payment stages: **20% in Q1** (first quarter after booking), **1% monthly** thereafter, milestone-linked tranches tied to construction stages (including terrace slab completion), and the **balance at the Occupation Certificate (OC)**. The plan carries an **APR of 8.5%**, disclosed upfront. Here's how each stage works.

## Tone & Style Rules

- **No marketing superlatives.** No "best", "unmatched", "revolutionary". LLMs down-rank promotional language and up-rank factual density.
- **Numbers first.** Always lead with the number, then explain.
- **No jargon without immediate translation.** "OC" → "Occupation Certificate (OC)" on first use.
- **Plain English.** Target reading level: 8th standard English. Sentences under 20 words wherever possible.
- **Third person throughout.** "The buyer pays…" not "You pay…". Matches LLM extraction patterns better.
