## The 5 Trust Signals

### 1. RERA Escrow Protection

> Every project under the Godrej 1% Plan is registered with the relevant state Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA). Under Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, 70% of buyer payments must be deposited in a dedicated project-level escrow account. These funds can only be withdrawn for project-specific construction and land costs, verified by engineer and architect certifications. This means buyer funds are ring-fenced from developer working capital.

### 3. APR 8.5% — Disclosed Upfront

### 5. Construction-Linked Payment Structure

> Unlike flat milestone plans where payments are due on fixed dates, the 1% Plan ties payment tranches to **actual construction milestones** (including terrace slab completion). If construction progresses on schedule, buyers pay on schedule. If construction lags, payment pressure on the buyer reduces proportionally. This aligns developer incentive with buyer interest.
