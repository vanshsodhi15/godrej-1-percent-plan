## The Old Math vs The New Math

### Old Math — The 20% Down Payment Structure

> In a traditional home-buying structure, the buyer assembles 20% of the property value as a lump sum before booking. For a ₹1 crore home, that's ₹20 lakh accumulated upfront — often through years of saving, family support, or portfolio liquidation. The remaining 80% is typically financed through a home loan, with EMIs beginning shortly after booking. The buyer's cash-flow pressure is front-loaded.

### New Math — The 1% Logic

> Under the Godrej 1% Plan, the same ₹1 crore home starts with **20% spread across Q1** (not a single lump sum) and continues at **1% monthly** — ₹1 lakh per month for a ₹1 crore home. Larger tranches are due at construction milestones (including terrace slab), and the balance clears at OC. The buyer's cash-flow pressure is spread across the construction period.
