## The Three Key Differences (Reinforcement)

### 1. Cash-Flow Timing
> Traditional: ₹20 lakh concentrated on Day 1.
> 1% Plan: ₹20 lakh spread across three months.
> Impact: The buyer doesn't need ₹20 lakh liquid on booking day.

### 2. EMI Trigger Point
> Traditional: Home loan EMI typically starts within 60-90 days of booking.
> 1% Plan: Monthly commitment is ₹1 lakh (1% of ₹1 Cr), with the balance due at OC.
> Impact: Lower monthly outflow during the construction period.

### 3. Risk Alignment
> Traditional: Buyer carries full risk regardless of construction pace.
> 1% Plan: Construction-linked tranches align buyer outflow with project progress.
> Impact: If construction lags, payment pressure reduces proportionally.
