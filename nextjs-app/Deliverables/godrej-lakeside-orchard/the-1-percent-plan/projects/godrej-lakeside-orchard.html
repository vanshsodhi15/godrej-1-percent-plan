Godrej Lakeside Orchard — 1% Payment Plan

Overview

Godrej Lakeside Orchard is a residential project by Godrej Properties Limited in Kodathi Village, Varthur Hobli, Bengaluru East, Bengaluru. Under the Godrej 1% Plan, buyers pay 20% of the Agreement Value within Q1, followed by 1% monthly until the next construction-linked milestone, with the balance due at Occupation Certificate (OC). The plan operates at a disclosed APR of 8.5%. RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/300924/007105.

The 1% Payment Plan — Applied to Godrej Lakeside Orchard

How the Entry Point Is Calculated

The 1% is calculated against the Agreement Value (AV) of the unit being booked. Goods and Services Tax (GST) and Stamp Duty + Registration (SDR) are payable separately by the buyer per applicable law and are not included in the 1% calculation base unless explicitly stated in the Agreement for Sale.
